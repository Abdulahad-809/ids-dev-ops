About the dataset

CICIDS 2017 was published by the Canadian Institute for Cybersecurity (University of New Brunswick). It contains five days of labelled traffic with benign activity and attacks such as DoS/DDoS, PortScan, brute force, botnet, infiltration and Heartbleed, captured as 78 CICFlowMeter flow features. Here, every attack class is merged into a single ATTACK label.
